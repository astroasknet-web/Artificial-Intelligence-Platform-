HOW IT WORKS

From first call to live agent.

Five steps, and you approve the wording before a customer hears it.

  1. 1

    Tell us the job

    Pick the conversation to automate: a first call, a follow-up, a reminder. We write down what a good outcome is.

  2. 2

    Teach it your way

    Share your scripts, answers and rules. The agent speaks from them and says so when it does not know.

  3. 3

    Test with your team

    Listen to sample calls and read sample chats. You approve the wording before a customer ever hears it.

  4. 4

    Go live, one channel first

    Start with a single channel and a small share of conversations, then widen.

  5. 5

    Review and improve

    Every conversation is logged. A person takes over when asked, and you tune the agent from real calls.

QUESTIONS

Good questions.

What is Yugty.AI?

A set of specialised AI agents. Some work for businesses, such as voice, WhatsApp, outbound calling, CRM updates and payment reminders. Others help individuals with their careers, such as resume feedback and mock interviews.

Do the agents replace my team?

No. They take the repetitive first contact and follow-up, and pass the conversation to a person when it matters.

Can I start with just one agent?

Yes. Most teams start with one channel, check the results, and then add more.

Which systems can the agents connect to?

Tell us what you use when you book a demo. We will say plainly what connects today and what does not.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the agents and the volume, so no prices are published here. Book a demo and we will send a quote.

Is my data used to train AI models?

No. Personal data is never used to train AI models.

Can I try the career agents myself?

Yes. The individual agents are for anyone. They open from the agents section once the agent site is connected.
